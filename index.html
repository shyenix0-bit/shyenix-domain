Returns and exchanges

Need a different size? Message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery with your order details and a photo. The piece must be unused and have its tags on.

Delivery

We confirm your order on WhatsApp, pack it, and ship it to your door. We’ll share the tracking details once it’s on its way.

Questions

How do I order?

Add pieces to your cart, press “Order on WhatsApp”, and send the message. We reply to confirm size, price and delivery.

Which size should I choose?

Look at the size guide and compare it with a shirt or pair of trousers that fits you well. If you’re between sizes, ask us on WhatsApp.

When do I pay?

After we confirm your order and the total with you on WhatsApp.

Can I exchange for another size?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery. The piece must be unused and have its tags on.

Is delivery free?

Delivery is free on orders above ₹999. For smaller orders, we tell you the delivery charge before you pay.
